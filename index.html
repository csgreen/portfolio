How I lead

I like complicated problems.
I just don't think the solutions need to be complicated.

01

Trust is the foundation.

In security, identity, and AI, the technically correct answer is only useful if people can rely on it.

02

Good work needs a system.

Clear goals, shared measures, and the right decision-making rhythms give teams room to do their best work.

03

Clarity creates momentum.

Bring the evidence, address the hardest objection, and help people move forward together.
