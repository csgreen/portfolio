How I lead

I like complicated problems.
I just don't think the solutions need to be complicated.

01

Make the complicated understandable.

I turn technical complexity into clear decisions without hiding risk, uncertainty, or tradeoffs.

02

Measure what changes, not what ships.

Adoption is the test. I look at what people actually do differently—not just what the team delivered.

03

Know what to cut.

I look for evidence before adding complexity, and remove work that doesn't serve the user or the goal.

04

Build teams that don't depend on me.

Clear priorities, decision rules, and coaching help people make sound decisions without constant intervention.
