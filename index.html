Current work · AI adoption and product practice

The AI adoption gap is a product problem.

AI tools are easy to try. Turning usage into measurable improvements in how teams work is harder. That's the problem I'm studying and teaching through Carrot & Craft.

I developed and presented Build Your AI Portfolio for the UW Foster HMBA Career Center in August 2026, connecting hands-on practice to evidence of real value. I've also taught software product management through UW's certificate program.
