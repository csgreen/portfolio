DocuSign · Identity and authentication

Modernizing access at DocuSign.

Identity choices have consequences far beyond the login screen. I owned roadmap and migration decisions for authentication serving large enterprise customers.

500Kenterprise accounts in the identity platform’s scope
20+engineering teams whose work intersected with identity
600K+users migrated across acquired login experiences

The problem

What needed to change

Multiple authentication experiences and legacy approaches created platform complexity, customer risk, and difficult cross-team coordination.

The product decision

Where I focused

Prioritize safer, more consistent access, including two-step verification and carefully managed legacy-authentication deprecation. Migration needed communication and support readiness as well as technical delivery.
