Designing adoption into security.

The problem

What needed to change

Partners administering SMB environments needed stronger delegated access controls, but security improvements can create complexity at precisely the point users need confidence.

The product decision

Where I focused

Treat partner setup as a product adoption problem, not simply a security-policy rollout. Make the right permissions easier to configure, while coordinating the underlying authorization changes across product teams.

What happened

Results and scale

The setup wizard reached 66% of monthly active users and became one of the platform’s five most engaging features. The broader authorization effort involved a seven-person core team and 15 workload teams.

This work taught me that compliance and usability belong in the same product decision. Adoption is part of delivering security—not a downstream marketing problem.
