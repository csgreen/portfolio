Making AI support genuinely useful.

A support agent is only valuable if it helps someone solve a problem. I worked on launched Microsoft Copilot support-agent experiences that achieved strong self-service outcomes.

The product problem

Move from an AI demo to useful support.

People seeking support need a clear, timely answer—not another step in the support journey. For an AI agent, speed alone is not enough; the meaningful outcome is whether users can resolve their issue without escalating.

What happened

Measure the outcome, not just the interaction.

Launched Copilot support-agent experiences achieved more than 70% self-service resolution, with response times under two minutes. These measures reflect the practical value delivered by the experiences, rather than simply counting conversations.

What the evidence supports

The honest scope.
